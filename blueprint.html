PART 5: YOUR FIRST DOLLAR ONLINE (the simple path)

There are 3 levels. Most men try to skip to Level 3 and make $0 forever. Don't.

Level 1: Freelance (weeks 1–8 after skill training starts)

Trade time for money. Fast cash, real experience.

  • Find 10 businesses per day that clearly need your skill (bad thumbnails, weak captions, dead landing pages).
  • Send a short, honest message:

"Hey [name] — I do [skill]. I noticed [specific problem]. I fixed/re-made one for you here [link]. If you like it, I'll do 3 more this month for $X. No pressure."

  • Price low at first ($50–150 per project). You're buying testimonials and skill, not a fortune.

Level 2: Productized service (months 2–4)

Same skill, fixed package, fixed price, fixed time.

"3 thumbnails per week, 48h turnaround, $300/month."

One offer. One price. Repeatable. This is how freelancers become business owners.

Level 3: Digital product (months 3–6+)

Package your knowledge once, sell it forever. Ebooks, templates, mini-courses, systems.

  • This is the real wealth lever: sell the same thing 100 times instead of selling your hours.
  • You don't need to be an expert with 10 years of experience. You need to be 3 steps ahead of the man behind you.

Your first $1,000 is usually this boring:

10 clients × $100 project = $1,000. Or 20 sales of a $50 product. That's it. No magic. Just volume and a system.
